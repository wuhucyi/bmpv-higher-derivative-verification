# Related work and imported boundaries

The following references identify the external results and conventions used
in the calculation.

- The two-derivative BMPV and Gutowski--Reall/STU solutions provide the
  stationary backgrounds and gauge-field conventions.
- [arXiv:2603.18180](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18180), especially its Eq. 4.1,
  anchors the positive sign convention for the five-dimensional potentials.
- [arXiv:2606.31987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31987), especially its Eq. 3.31,
  supplies a rotating heterotic solution used for a scoped solution-space
  comparison.
- [arXiv:2605.19552](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19552), especially Eqs. 3.7 and
  3.8, is used in the entropy-convention comparison. Agreement is assessed
  only after matching the ensemble, charges, and spin variables.
- [arXiv:2604.24658](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24658) motivates the
  Agent-Native Research Artifact organization. The present symbolic-physics
  structure is ARA-inspired and is not represented as formal certification.

Every imported equation remains convention-dependent until checked against
[`concepts.md`](concepts.md). No source citation replaces executable evidence.
