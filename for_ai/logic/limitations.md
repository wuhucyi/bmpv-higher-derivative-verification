# Scientific scope and limitations

1. The five-dimensional result is complete only through first order in
   `lambda3`, or `alphaPrime` after `lambda3 = alphaPrime/8`.
2. Exactness in rotation applies to the stationary equal-angular-momentum BMPV
   ansatz, not to a generic two-spin or non-BPS ansatz.
3. Regularity and entropy apply for positive charges and
   `j^2 < mu_1 mu_2 mu_3`; the classical zero-area endpoint is excluded.
4. The rotating paper-coordinate construction is a useful pullback, not a
   proof of a unique fully isotropic rotating gauge.
5. Entropy is stated in the fixed physical Page-charge ensemble. Formulas at
   fixed harmonic parameters or with different spin variables need not agree
   term by term.
6. Vanishing of the separate curvature-free vector invariants, including
   their `F^4` terms, does not permit deleting vector completion terms from the
   active supersymmetric curvature-squared invariant.
7. The rotating scalar and third-vector field maps are proved on the BMPV
   solution space through first order and exact in rotation.
8. No unique manifestly covariant off-shell origin of the extra third-vector
   shift has been derived. NB32 establishes insufficiency and nonuniqueness for
   the displayed source structures, not a universal no-go theorem.
9. NB27 preserves a nonclosed three-form-first endpoint as a rejected route;
   it is superseded at solution level by the raw-potential-first NB31 result.
10. NB00 contains resource-bounded derivation stages. NB12 is the decisive
    literal from-EOM exact-in-rotation verifier.
11. Reproduction requires a compatible licensed Wolfram kernel. NB01 and NB02
    are stored losslessly as `.nb.gz`; restore them only after checking the
    recorded compressed and uncompressed hashes.
