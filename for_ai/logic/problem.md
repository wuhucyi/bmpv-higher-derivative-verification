# Problem

## Scientific question

Determine and independently verify the correction to the generic three-charge
BMPV black hole that is first order in the active supersymmetric
four-derivative coupling and exact in the single equal-angular-momentum
rotation parameter within the stationary ansatz.

The calculation must keep three unequal charge parameters, solve the complete
linearized five-dimensional equations, fix the physical charge and
angular-momentum normalization, establish regularity on the perturbative
branch, compute entropy in the correct ensemble, and separate the
five-dimensional solution from the stronger heterotic-to-STU field-map
question.

## Acceptance standard

A generic claim requires exact arbitrary-charge substitution into the original
reduced equations or an equivalent independently reconstructed identity.
Finite samples may guide discovery or expose an error but cannot prove a
generic identity. Exactness in rotation additionally requires a justified
maximum source degree and vanishing of every allowed residual coefficient.

## Out of scope

The artifact does not establish second order in the higher-derivative
coupling, a generic unequal-two-spin or non-BPS solution, the classical
zero-area endpoint, a unique rotating isotropic gauge, or a unique universal
off-shell heterotic-to-STU field redefinition on unrelated backgrounds.
